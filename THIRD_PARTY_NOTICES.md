# Third-party data

## HASYv2

`assets/operator_images/hasy/` contains a subset of operator-symbol images
derived from the **HASYv2 handwritten symbol database**, created by Martin
Thoma.

- Dataset: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.259444
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.08380
- License: ODC Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0
  (https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)

The HASYv2 attribution and license apply to those images. They are not covered
by the repository's MIT license.
